Getting Started with UNILIS

UNILIS (University Learning Integrated System) simplifies campus communication, academic management, and digital learning. Whether you're a student, lecturer, or admin — the system is designed to keep your university experience organized, accessible, and interactive.

👨‍🎓 Students

Access course notes, submit assignments, attend online meetings, and check grades — all in one platform.

👩‍🏫 Lecturers

Upload notes, create assignments, track submissions, schedule meetings, and manage unit performance effortlessly.

🏛️ Admin

Manage universities, departments, courses, units, and users while maintaining full control over academic data.
